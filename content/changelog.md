# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial public release.

- Three presets: Quiet Luxury, Clinical Skincare and Botanical & Organic, plus two alternative homepage layouts.
- Neutral, complete default homepage, footer and sample journal posts.
- Collection filters with active-filter chips, quick add, and product swatches, low-stock warning, trust badges and cross-sell on product pages.
- Cart drawer with free shipping progress, product suggestions, discount code field and order note.
- Mega menu, instant-search overlay, country and language selector.
- Section library: hero, autoplay video, featured collection, collection list, image with text, icon benefits, logo list, testimonials, before and after slider, shoppable image, FAQ, journal, promo countdown, newsletter.
- Cookie banner, optional exit-intent popup, complete JSON-LD structured data.
- All customer account, password, gift card, blog, article and contact templates.
