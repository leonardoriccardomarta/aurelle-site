# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial public release.

- Clinical Skincare style (white, cool grey and pastel blue, technical sans-serif), plus an alternative homepage layout.
- Neutral, complete default homepage, footer and sample journal posts.
- Collection filters with active-filter chips, quick add, and product swatches, low-stock warning, trust badges and cross-sell on product pages.
- Cart drawer and cart page with free shipping progress, product suggestions, applied discounts, order note, tax note and accelerated checkout buttons on the cart page.
- Mega menu, instant-search overlay, country and language selector.
- Section library: hero, autoplay video, featured collection, collection list, image with text, icon benefits, logo list, testimonials, image comparison, shoppable image, FAQ, journal, promo countdown, newsletter.
- Featured product and Custom Liquid sections, and a Custom Liquid block in the product page and in most sections.
- Product page: native swatches, selling plan selector, Shop Pay Installments, pickup availability, gift card recipient, tax note and unit prices.
- Header account component, Follow on Shop button, filters and sorting on search results.
- Complete JSON-LD structured data.
- All customer account, password, gift card, blog, article and contact templates.
